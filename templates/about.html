About FireGuard AI 🔥

Building intelligent fire and smoke detection systems using artificial intelligence to protect lives and property.

Our Mission 🎯

To prevent fire-related disasters through fast, accurate and reliable AI technology.

FireGuard AI leverages computer vision and deep learning to detect fire and smoke at an early stage, enabling timely action before damage spreads.

Traditional fire detection systems often react late. Our AI-based solution continuously analyzes visual data from cameras to identify dangerous patterns in real time, improving accuracy while reducing false alarms.

System Architecture & Technology Stack 🧩

Core components and technologies powering FireGuard AI

🔧 Architecture Overview

FireGuard AI follows a modular architecture where live camera feeds or uploaded videos are processed frame-by-frame using deep learning models. The system analyzes visual patterns to detect fire and smoke in real time and triggers alerts when a threat is identified.

  • 📷 Input: CCTV / Webcam / Video Upload
  • 🧠 AI Model: CNN-based Fire & Smoke Detection
  • ⚙️ Backend: Python + Flask
  • 📊 Output: Detection Status & Alerts
🧠
Deep Learning

Convolutional Neural Networks trained on fire and smoke datasets.

🐍
Python & Flask

Backend framework handling inference, routing and APIs.

🎥
Computer Vision

OpenCV used for video stream processing and frame analysis.
